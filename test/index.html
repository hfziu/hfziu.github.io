Math

Math rendering is supported by KaTeX.

The following code (believe it or not, the backslash \ has to be escaped):

\begin{equation}
\begin{bmatrix}
   a & b \\\\
   c & d
\end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}

produces

\begin{equation} \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} \end{equation}
